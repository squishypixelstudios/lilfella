# Growing Up and the Evolution Tree

## Stages and ages

| Stage | When |
| --- | --- |
| **Lil Blob** (baby) | From the dropper, for the first hour |
| **Lil Blub** | At 1 hour old, once all seven lessons are learned (see [First-Hour Lessons](First-Hour-Lessons.md)) |
| **Lil Blunkus** | At 1 day old |
| **Lil Fella** | At 3 days old |
| **Big Blorbus** | From 8 days old, for some looks |

Before each new stage your fella pulses bigger and smaller among stars. Then you pick the look: the screen says "Pick new form:", arrows flip through the options, and you tap the fella to choose. Until you choose, they keep their old look.

Growing up waits until your fella is awake with the light on. A stage reached during sleep starts when they wake.

## Your choices shape the path

Each pick limits the next. The picks run in order: Lil Blub, then Lil Blunkus, then Lil Fella, then (for some looks) Big Blorbus.

### Lil Blub: pick one

- **Blobbert**
- **Mochi**
- **Cheeks**

### Lil Blunkus: depends on the Lil Blub

- After **Blobbert**: Sprout, Bun, Curly or Slosh.
- After **Mochi**: Wobbles, Mallow or Toasty.
- After **Cheeks**: Bean, Bun or Slosh.

### Lil Fella: depends on the Lil Blunkus

- **Sprout** (from any Lil Blub): Jelly, SpikyBoi or Marshy.
- **Bun**, after Blobbert or Mochi: Puddle, Jelly or Puff.
- **Bun**, after Cheeks: Dapper, Puff or Fubz.
- **Curly**: Fubz, Boop or SpikyBoi.
- **Slosh**, after Blobbert or Mochi: Nimbus, Boop or Ballboi.
- **Slosh**, after Cheeks: Nimbus, Boop, Ballboi, Dapper or Fubz.
- **Wobbles**: Jelly, Puddle or Nimbus.
- **Mallow**: Loaf, Puff or Boop.
- **Toasty**: Loaf or Marshy.
- **Bean** (only after Cheeks): Dapper, SpikyBoi, Marshy or Fubz.

So the eleven Lil Fella looks are: Dapper, Loaf, Puddle, Jelly, SpikyBoi, Puff, Marshy, Boop, Fubz, Nimbus and Ballboi.

### Big Blorbus

From 8 days old, some looks are offered a Big Blorbus form. You can also turn it down and keep the form you have:

- **Jelly** or **Puff** from the Blobbert line: **Big Blob** (Big Blobbert).
- **Ballboi** from the Cheeks line: **Big Ball** (Big Ballboi).
- **Nimbus** or **Dapper**: **Alien Cat in a Tuxedo**.

Big Blorbus never gets hungry. Its Feed icon is the ORBIT CATCH game (see [Mini-Games](Mini-Games.md)).

## Hidden breed

At one day old, the care your fella has had decides a hidden breed. It changes them a little under the hood. One breed is drawn filled in dark grey.

## Where the looks are kept

The Journal records every look you have raised, by stage (see [Old Friends and Journal](Old-Friends-and-Journal.md)).
