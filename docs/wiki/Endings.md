# Endings

Every life ends the same way: your fella melts into a puddle, and the dropper takes them back. What causes it is one of five things.

## The five endings

### Recycled

A well-kept Lil Fella, after about a month. Poor care ages a fella faster, so the best-kept fellas live the longest. This is the happy ending: the screen says BYE BYE or THANK YOU, with sparkles.

### Overfed

Fed far too much for far too long. There is a hidden limit. Feed only when the HUNGRY call comes, and the meal is refused when they are completely full.

### Too sick

Health runs out. Each untreated sickness wears health down. One sickness on its own takes about 30 awake hours to finish a fella, and it is faster when sicknesses stack or the mess piles up. Health only drops while awake, so a night's sleep is always safe.

**How to avoid it:** use Medicine as soon as they are sick, and keep the light off at night (see [Caring for Your Fella](Caring-for-Your-Fella.md)).

### Too sad

Twelve awake hours in a row at zero happiness. At six hours you get a warning sound. Cheering them up to half happy clears the count.

**How to avoid it:** play with them and pet them (see [Mini-Games](Mini-Games.md)).

### Lost trust

Trust runs out. (This ending used to be called "ran away".) Ignoring your fella builds up resentment slowly. A totally ignored fella lasts about three days before leaving. Petting them reduces resentment.

**How to avoid it:** do not leave your fella alone for days. Keep them fed, clean and played with.

## What happens when a fella ends

1. The caption appears: BYE BYE or THANK YOU (with sparkles) for a Recycled ending, otherwise OH NO and the reason.
2. Your fella slowly melts into a puddle.
3. The dropper comes down over the puddle and waits. PUSH blinks on the screen.
4. Squeeze the dropper and let go. It sucks up a part of the puddle. Do this three times to suck up all of it.
5. The dropper rises back up. About three seconds after the last squeeze, the name screen opens on its own. You do not need to shake the device or tap anything.
6. Name the new blob, then tap **Let's goo!**
7. The opening scene shows the dropper again. Tap once to squeeze out the new blob.

The fella's record goes into [Old Friends and Journal](Old-Friends-and-Journal.md), with their look, name, ending, and age.
