# LOCK

The **Lock** icon (padlock, bottom row) lets you freeze your fella in time.

## Locking

Tap the padlock. The screen asks **LOCK?** with YES and NO. Choose YES.

While locked:

- your fella stays exactly as they are: they do not age,
- they do not grow into the next stage,
- they cannot die,
- the padlock icon stays lit.

Everything else carries on. They still get hungry, messy, sick and sleepy, and they still call for you.

## Unlocking

Tap the padlock again. The screen asks **UNLOCK?**. Choose YES. Time carries on from where it stopped.

## Note

Lock is not one of the first-hour lessons (see [First-Hour Lessons](First-Hour-Lessons.md)).
