# Mini-Games

Mini-games live behind the **Play** icon (four games) and the **Clean** icon (the scrub). Each one raises happiness by how well you did, up to 4 hearts. A game left untouched for 30 seconds goes back to the home screen.

## MOVE

Things fall from the ceiling. Slide your finger anywhere on the screen to move your fella out of their way. One hit ends the game. Around 27 to 30 seconds is a perfect run.

## RUN

A side-scroller. Tap to jump, and tap again in the air for a double jump. A card before the run reminds you. Power-ups turn up in a different place each time.

## YEET

Press anywhere and drag back to stretch the sling. A dotted line shows the way. Let go and fling your fella. Trampolines, springs, bumpers and gusts help. Rocks, birds, clouds and mud do not. Your score is in metres, and your best is saved.

## POP

A bubble shooter, played on the whole screen. Bubbles hang from the ceiling; slide a finger left and right to aim (a dotted line shows the way, wall bounces included) and let go to fire. Three or more of a kind touching pop, and whatever hung from them drops off. Every 8 shots the ceiling comes down a row, and a bubble pushed below the line ends the game. The full guide is on the [POP](POP.md) page.

## Clean (the scrub)

This is not a play game but it works like one. Rub your finger back and forth over the long litter box and the floor mess. The poop nearest your finger wears away. Every poop is a different shape, and some are stuck on (crusty, or with flies), which takes up to twice the rubbing. One easy poop takes about two seconds of brisk rubbing, and each further one about a second more. Leave the scrub and come back, and it is as you left it. You cannot fail.

## Orbit Catch (Big Blorbus)

Big Blorbus does not eat. Its Feed icon opens ORBIT CATCH: eat the planets and dodge the spiky ones. Three or more hearts earns a snack.
