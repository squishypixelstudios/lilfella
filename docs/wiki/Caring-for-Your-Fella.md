# Caring for Your Fella

Your fella has a few needs. When one needs something, the **Attention** icon lights up and says what it is (HUNGRY, BORED, MESSY, SLEEPY, SICK, CHECK ME). Tap it to see the call, then take care of it.

## Hunger

Your fella gets hungry over time. Tap **Feed** to serve a meal. A completely full fella refuses food. A hungry fella wakes up for food even if asleep.

If the belly runs completely empty, your fella gets sick (see below).

## Fun and happiness

Your fella gets bored over time. Two things cheer them up:

- **Play**: the mini-games raise happiness by how well you did (up to 4 hearts). See [Mini-Games](Mini-Games.md).
- **Petting**: tap your fella.

A fella who stays at zero happiness for a long time becomes too sad to live. See [Endings](Endings.md).

## Mess

Your fella makes poop. Poops need something in the tummy first, and at night they hold it until morning. When there is poop in the litter box, the box appears in the bottom right. Tap it to open the Clean game and scrub the poop away. Some poops are stuck on (crusty, or with flies), and take up to twice the rubbing. You cannot fail at cleaning. Floor mess is cleaned the same way.

## Sickness and medicine

There are no random sicknesses. Your fella gets sick when:

- the belly runs completely empty, or
- the light stays on through their night's sleep (see below).

A sick fella looks speckled and droopy, with an icon by the head for each kind of sickness (germ, crossed-out bowl, lit bulb). A number appears when there is more than one.

Each sickness drains health while your fella is awake. Give medicine with the **Medicine** icon: one dose cures one sickness. If there are several, the screen says "1 MORE" until the last one. The dose works at once, and the animation cannot be cut short. Medicine is refused when your fella is not sick.

Untreated sickness is the most common way fellas die. See [Endings](Endings.md).

## Light and sleep

Your fella goes to bed at night. The times are:

| Stage | Bedtime | Wakes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lil Blob | 8pm | 10am |
| Lil Blub | 9pm | 9am |
| Lil Blunkus | 9:30pm | 8:30am |
| Lil Fella | 10pm | 8am |

When they fall asleep they want the light off (the SLEEPY call). Leaving the light on for 15 minutes is a care mistake. Leaving it on for more than 15% of the night's sleep makes them sick when they wake.

Asleep in the dark they rest better. They wake in the morning and the light comes back on. A fella woken in the night to be fed keeps the light off.

There is no energy level and no tiredness: every game is free, as often as you like, and turning the light off in the day does not send them to sleep. The one daytime nap is the Lil Blob's test nap (see [First-Hour Lessons](First-Hour-Lessons.md)).

## Attention calls

These are the things your fella can ask for:

- **HUNGRY**: time to eat.
- **BORED**: time to play.
- **MESSY**: poop to clean up.
- **SLEEPY**: turn the light off.
- **SICK**: time for medicine.
- **CHECK ME**: a check-up call.
- **HELLO!**: a greeting, mostly in the first hour.

Tap the **Attention** icon to see the call, and tap the matching icon to answer it.

## Care mistakes

If one of the needs calls (hungry, bored, messy, sleepy or sick) is left unanswered for 15 minutes, that counts as one care mistake, and another for each further hour it is left. The game keeps the count. Starter-bug calls in the first hour do not count (see [First-Hour Lessons](First-Hour-Lessons.md)).

## How often things happen

Upkeep is busiest at the start and eases off as your fella grows:

| Stage | Poops | Good moments | From full to empty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The first hour | about every 3 minutes at first | 4-5 | about an hour |
| Lil Blub | about every 6 minutes | every 30-50 minutes | 2-3 hours |
| Lil Blunkus | about every 10 minutes | every 50-80 minutes | about 5 hours |
| Lil Fella | about every 15 minutes | every 80-140 minutes | about 9 hours |

The first days need the most looking after, because hunger drains fastest then.

## Being away

Turning the device off never ends a life. While you are away, the fella's needs keep changing, but gently, and they will not die while you are gone.
