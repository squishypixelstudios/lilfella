# The Screen and Icons

The game screen is a small grid of dots in four shades of grey, with eight icons around it. Your fella lives in the middle.

## The eight icons

Four icons run along the top, and four along the bottom.

**Top row (left to right):** Feed, Light, Play, Clean (the scoop, top right).

**Bottom row (left to right):** Meter (bottom left), Medicine, Lock, Attention.

| Icon | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Feed** (fish and bowl) | Serves a meal. Refused when your fella is completely full. Big Blorbus does not eat: this icon becomes a star game (see [Mini-Games](Mini-Games.md)). |
| **Light** (bulb) | Switches the light off or on. Your fella sleeps at bedtime and wants the light off. |
| **Play** (yarn) | Opens the four mini-games: MOVE, RUN, YEET and POP (see [Mini-Games](Mini-Games.md)). |
| **Clean** (scoop) | Opens the clean-up game for the litter box and the mess on the floor (see [Mini-Games](Mini-Games.md)). |
| **Medicine** (hollow plus) | Gives one dose of medicine, which cures one sickness. Refused when your fella is not sick. |
| **Meter** (scales) | Shows the stats page (see below). |
| **Lock** (padlock) | Asks LOCK? (see [LOCK](LOCK.md)). |
| **Attention** (blob with an exclamation mark) | Lights up when your fella needs something. Tap it to see what. Nothing shows when they are happy. |

## The meter page

Tap the **Meter** icon, then tap the screen to move through the pages. The first page shows the name, age and battery charge. The next pages show:

- **HUNGRY**: how full the belly is (hearts).
- **HAPPY**: how happy your fella is (hearts).
- **TRUST**: how much your fella trusts you (hearts).
- **GIFTS**: how many treasures this fella has brought you (see [Exploring and Gifts](Exploring-and-Gifts.md)).
- **SICK**: how many sicknesses they have had.
- The stage they are in.

## Other things on the screen

- **Tap your fella** to pet them.
- **The litter box** (bottom right) appears only while there is poop in it waiting to be cleaned. Tap it to clean up.
- **Tap an empty spot** to see the time. This works in the dark too: any tap on the screen shows it.
- **Hold the screen for about one second** to open [Settings](Settings.md). This works in the middle of an animation or an ending too; it carries on from the same spot after Back.
- A game left untouched for 30 seconds goes back to the home screen.
