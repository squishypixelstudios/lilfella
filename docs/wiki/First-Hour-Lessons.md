# First-Hour Lessons

Like the 2nd-generation Tamagotchi, your Lil Blob teaches you how to care for it. Every few minutes, while your fella is awake, it asks for something new you have not done yet:

| Call | Icon to answer |
| --- | --- |
| HUNGRY | Feed |
| SLEEPY | Light |
| BORED | Play |
| SICK | Medicine |
| MESSY | Clean |
| CHECK ME | Meter |
| HELLO! | Attention |

Lock is not a lesson.

Two more things come on a schedule:

- **A starter bug** in the first five minutes. Cure it with the medicine. This one is a lesson only: it never counts as a care mistake.
- **A sleepy nap** at 6 to 9 minutes old. Turn the light off while they sleep.

Those two only count when you do them for real. If you miss them, they come round again until you do.

When every one of the seven icons has been used for its lesson, the Lil Blob says "ALL LEARNED" and the lessons are over.

The Lil Blob only grows into a Lil Blub after the seven care icons have been used (Lock is not one of them), and once it is an hour old.
