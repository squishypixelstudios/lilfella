# Getting Started

## 1. The title screen

On a brand new device the title screen comes first. All ten Lil Fellas parade past to a looping tune. Tap to start.

## 2. Set the clock

Set the date and time. The clock matters: the game uses it for bedtime, and it shows the time when you tap an empty spot.

## 3. Name your fella

Type a name on the keyboard (up to 10 letters), or tap **Random name** to get one from the list. Then tap **Let's goo!**

## 4. Squeeze the dropper

After "Let's goo!" the opening scene shows a lab eyedropper. Tap the screen once to squeeze out the new blob. A drop falls a long way, splats on the floor, and settles as your **Lil Blob**.

## What to do next

- Take a look at [The Screen and Icons](The-Screen-and-Icons.md) to learn the eight buttons around the screen.
- Follow the [First-Hour Lessons](First-Hour-Lessons.md): your Lil Blob will ask for each kind of care in turn.
- Read [Caring for Your Fella](Caring-for-Your-Fella.md) for what each need means.

## Turning the screen on and off

- The screen turns off when the device is left untouched (see [Settings](Settings.md) for how long).
- Turn it back on with a tap, or with the PWR or BOOT button. Picking the device up does not wake it.
- Things that happen while the screen is off (a poop, growing up, an ending) wait for you when you look.

## Starting over

To start from scratch, use **Erase everything** on the third settings page (see [Settings](Settings.md)). This removes every fella, your old friends and your settings.
