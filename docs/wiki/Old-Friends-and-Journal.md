# Old Friends and Journal

Both are in [Settings](Settings.md) on page 2.

## Old Friends

The **Old friends** list remembers the last eight fellas that made it out of the dropper. Newest first, each entry shows:

- their look,
- their name,
- how their life ended (see [Endings](Endings.md)),
- how old they were.

Four entries show per page. A fella who ended before the drop was complete is not recorded.

## Journal

The **Journal** shows every look in the game, in stages. A look you have raised shows its picture. Looks you have not raised yet show a question mark.

Tap a look to see its name. Tap a look you have not raised, and it still shows as ???. The count at the side of the Journal entry tells you how many you have found out of the total.
