# Exploring and Gifts

From **Lil Blunkus** (1 day old) on, your fella sometimes slips out exploring. While they are out, a goo trail wobbles off the floor. When they come back, they may have a treasure for you.

## When exploring starts

A trip only starts when all of these are true:

- your fella is at least Lil Blunkus (1 day old),
- they are awake,
- their belly is not nearly empty, their happiness is not low, and their health is not low,
- there is no treasure already waiting on the floor.

Trips never start while the device is off.

When those are true, a timer counts down. The first timer is random, between 3 and 6 hours of time. After each trip, the next one is random, between 1.5 and 4 hours. The timer only counts down while those conditions hold.

## During a trip

- The fella is out. Feed, Medicine and the other icons say "OUT".
- A trip lasts about 2 to 4 minutes.
- The fella does not poop while they are out.
- You cannot pet them while they are out.

## Coming back with a gift

When the trip ends, there is a chance of a treasure. The chance depends on health: the healthier your fella, the more likely. It is between about 55% (when health is very low) and 95% (when health is full).

If there is a treasure, it is one of:

| Treasure | Chance |
| --- | --- |
| Flower | 45% |
| Shell | 25% |
| Pebble | 15% |
| Berry | 10% |
| Gem (rare) | 5% |

A "GIFT!" message and a sound tell you a treasure has come back. If there is no treasure, the fella just returns, with no message.

## Collecting it

The treasure appears on the floor at the bottom left of the screen. **Tap it** to take it. The screen says "THANKS!", you get a little happiness, and the GIFTS count goes up by one.

A treasure that is not collected lasts about 15 minutes of awake time, then disappears. While the device is off it waits for you.

## Gifts while you are away

If the device is off, a treasure can still appear on the floor now and then, once the timer runs out. These are a little less varied: flower, shell, pebble or berry, with no gem. No trip happens while the device is off.

## What a gift does

A gift does not change anything in the game. It is a trophy: the number is counted for that fella and shown on the Meter page as **GIFTS**. (The number stops counting at 999.)

The count is kept with that fella. When they end, the count is saved in the [Old Friends and Journal](Old-Friends-and-Journal.md) list, but that list does not show it.
