# FAQ

**My fella will not eat.**
They may be out exploring (the screen says OUT), asleep and not hungry enough to wake, or completely full (a full fella refuses food). See [Exploring and Gifts](Exploring-and-Gifts.md) and [Caring for Your Fella](Caring-for-Your-Fella.md).

**I cannot pet my fella.**
They may be out exploring, or asleep. See [Exploring and Gifts](Exploring-and-Gifts.md).

**What is the GIFTS number?**
The count of treasures this fella has brought back. It does not do anything else. See [Exploring and Gifts](Exploring-and-Gifts.md).

**My fella left a treasure but it disappeared.**
Treasures stay on the floor for about 15 minutes of awake time. Tap them to take them before they vanish.

**Why did my fella die?**
See [Endings](Endings.md). Each ending has its own cause and way to avoid it, so check the reason shown on the screen.

**Can I turn the sound off?**
Yes: in [Settings](Settings.md), set Sound to 0.

**How do I see the time?**
Tap an empty spot on the screen. It works in the dark too.

**How do I open Settings?**
Hold the screen for about one second.

**Does the device have to be picked up to wake it?**
No. Tap the screen or press the PWR or BOOT button. Picking the device up does not wake it.

**Does turning the device off end my fella's life?**
No. Being away never ends a life.

**How do I start over?**
In [Settings](Settings.md), page 3, choose **Erase everything**. This removes every fella and all settings.

**Where is the Lock?**
The padlock is on the bottom row. See [LOCK](LOCK.md).
