# Settings

Open Settings by holding the screen for about one second. This works on the home screen, in the middle of any animation, and during an ending. The settings have three pages. Tap a row to change it, or to open it. Move between the pages with **More** and **Back**.

## Page 1

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Sound** | The volume, from **0** (off) to **5** (max). Each tap is one step quieter (2, 1, 0 OFF), then round to 5 MAX. A new save starts at 2. |
| **Brightness** | Cycles through five levels. |
| **Screen** | Switches between **LCD** (the classic grey) and **NIGHT** (inverted colours). |
| **Auto off** | How long the screen waits before turning off when untouched. The steps are 5 Sec, 15 Sec, 30 Sec (the default), 1 Min, and Never. An animation is never cut off part-way. Never keeps the screen on, but it dims after 45 seconds untouched. |
| **More** | Goes to page 2. |

## Page 2

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Old friends** | The fellas you have raised before. See [Old Friends and Journal](Old-Friends-and-Journal.md). |
| **Journal** | Every look you have raised, by stage. See [Old Friends and Journal](Old-Friends-and-Journal.md). |
| **Clock** | Sets the date and time. |
| **Speed** | A developer option that speeds up time for testing: x1, x1000, x3000. The code marks it for removal before the final version. |
| **More** | Goes to page 3. |

## Page 3

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **New blob** | Starts a new blob (after a confirm). Your old fella is gone. |
| **Erase everything** | Removes every fella, your old friends and all settings, and starts over from the first-start clock (after a confirm). |
| **Touch** | Off, or DEBUG: draws a ring under your finger where the screen thinks you touched. Useful for setting up a board. |
| **About** | Who made the game, and the version. |
| **Back** | Returns to the page before, or to the home screen. |

The two options that wipe things (New blob and Erase everything) are on their own page so they are harder to hit by mistake.
