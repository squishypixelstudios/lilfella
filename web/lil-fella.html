How to play

  • Icons: feed, light (a switch), play, clean (top); medicine, meter, lock, attention (bottom).
  • Lock (the padlock): keeps your Lil Fella just as they are. No growing up, no dying; they still need looking after. Tap it again to unlock.
  • Clean: rub your finger back and forth over the mess to scrub it away.
  • Sick? They get sick when they go hungry right down to empty, or when the light stays on through their night's sleep. One dose of medicine per sickness.
  • Tap the fella to pet them, and tap found treasures to collect them.
  • Tap an empty spot on the LCD to see the time.
  • Hold the LCD for 1 second to open Settings (Old friends, Erase everything and more).
  • When the attention icon lights up, tap it to see why.
